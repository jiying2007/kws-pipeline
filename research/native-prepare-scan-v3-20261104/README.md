## Output-accounting correction

The old is_file-then-stat race is replaced with the reviewed fd-anchored,
no-follow streaming scanner. Vanished or changed entries invalidate the entire
sum, permitting at most five full attempts within a0.25-second observation budget.
Persistent mutation and non-ENOENT I/O errors fail closed. Logical file-size
threshold remains exactly64MiB. A final post-exit check is required before success.
Symlinks, hardlinked files, special files, cross-device descendants, excessive
depth and entry counts are rejected. Existing supervisor failure handling and
cleanup of its one confirmed-created container remain unchanged.

This is polling, not a kernel quota or an atomic whole-filesystem snapshot. The
userspace budget is checked between syscalls and before accepting the observation;
it is not a kernel I/O timeout. The host-side output ancestors/root name are not
replaceable by the container; only contents beneath the dedicated output mount
are writable. Full child traversal is fd-anchored.
