## What is preserved / 保留边界

- Production streaming decoder, acoustic model, keyword pack, thresholds and shipping approval are unchanged.
- The eight output fixes are hosted CLI reliability fixes, not acoustic changes.
- Historical ASR artifacts, one-shot authorizations and retired workflows are not rewritten or re-enabled.
- The earlier 39 saved replays retain their original source/binary/input/output identities. New tools require new build receipts; old results are not rebound to repaired binaries.
- Native A20 original source identities remain recoverable through the consolidation archive. Current maintained source is explicitly distinguished from original byte-identical source.
- Real AFE controlled-runner checks do not claim protection against arbitrary concurrent same-user processes.

## Verification boundaries / 验证边界

Tests use source-only fixtures, invented logits/PCM and explicit test-only providers.
They do not establish real speech, real AFE execution, target-board performance,
checkpoint export parity or a new trained model. Direct compiler checks and
sanitizers are distinct from the hosted full CMake/CTest matrix; consult the PR
and exact main commit's Actions results for the latter.

Promotion tests execute complete fake bundle and ZIP validation with opaque bytes,
not downloaded models, training, attestation or a release. CI trigger changes do
not modify the governed training request. Existing CPU objective-math regressions
remain tests, not a new data/model experiment.
